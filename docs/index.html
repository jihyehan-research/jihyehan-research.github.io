About me

I am a PhD student in Health Policy at Vanderbilt University.

I hold a PharmD from Chung-Ang University in South Korea and an MPH in Health Policy from the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.

Previously, I worked with Program on Regulation, Therapeutics, And Law (PORTAL) at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School, following experience in regulatory affairs at GSK Seoul office.
