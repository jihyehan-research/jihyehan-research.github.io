01 / Research

Questions

01

Pharmaceutical Regulation

How do regulators like the US FDA balance regulatory flexibility with rigorous evidentiary standards for innovative new drugs?

Early access to innovative medicines such as first-in-class drugs can benefit patients with life-threatening conditions; however, these drugs may carry clinical uncertainty when approved on the basis of incomplete clinical evidence or surrogate endpoints. I study how the regulators’ approval decisions can optimize clinical outcomes for patients by balancing regulatory flexibility with rigorous assessment of the evidence supporting pharmaceutical innovation.

Keywords: first-in-class drug, accelerated approval, rare disease treatment

02

Medication Access & Affordability

How can pharmaceutical pricing and reimbursement policies ensure affordable and sustainable prescription drug coverage?

Pharmacy benefit design—situated within a complex insurance structure that, in the US, is segmented across private insurance and public programs—determines patient access to prescription drugs. I study the mechanisms that promote or thwart effective market competition, along with the factors that shape medication access and affordability. I analyze a wide variety of relevant datasets, including commercial claims, Medicare drug formularies, and Center for Medicare and Medicaid (CMS) spending data.

Keywords: claim data analysis, pharmacy benefit design, pharmacoepidemiology

03

Health Decision Science

How can policymakers allocate limited resources to maximize societal benefit and equitable access to high-value care?

From a health system perspective, resources are limited, and every policy decision carries trade-offs. In particular, as new technologies accelerate to address unmet medical needs and health care spending rises with costly new medicines, our ability to prioritize equitable access to high-value care is at stake. I study the evidence that informs policymakers about the expected downstream effects of their decisions on patients, as well as optimization methods that can deliver the greatest societal benefit.

Keywords: pharmacoeconomics, health decision modeling, cost-effectiveness analysis
