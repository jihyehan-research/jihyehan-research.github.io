About me

Research informed by a background in pharmacy.

I am a PhD student in Health Policy at Vanderbilt University and a graduate researcher at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

I hold an MPH in Health Policy from Harvard and a PharmD from Chung-Ang University. Previously, I worked with PORTAL at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School, following experience in regulatory affairs at GSK.
